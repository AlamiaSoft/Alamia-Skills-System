# SEO Content Optimization

Use to review an existing draft or page against a target query.

## Inputs
draft/page, target query, audience, purpose and brand voice

## Procedure
assess intent match and topical coverage; review title, H1, headings, introduction, internal links and metadata; identify only reader-useful gaps; recommend specific edits and validation

## Outputs
 prioritized recommendations, suggested title/meta, revised passages and checklist

## Quality checks
 natural language, evidence-led claims and no irrelevant keyword insertion

## Boundaries
 preserve factual meaning; do not publish without approval.
