# Product Specification

## Purpose
Turn an approved product direction into a PRD, user stories, acceptance criteria and prioritized requirements.

## Activation
Turn an approved product direction into a PRD, user stories, acceptance criteria and prioritized requirements.

## Inputs
Discovery brief; users; MVP scope; constraints; product outcomes; stakeholder decisions.

## Procedure
1. Translate outcomes into journeys and functional requirements.
2. Write user stories with observable acceptance criteria and negative/error paths.
3. Define roles, permissions, tenant boundaries, data ownership, audit and lifecycle states.
4. Specify security, privacy, performance, availability, accessibility, localization, recovery and compatibility.
5. Define integration contracts, retries, idempotency, retention, analytics events and KPIs.
6. Prioritize requirements and trace them to tests and tasks.

## Outputs
PRD; journeys; prioritized requirements; acceptance criteria; NFRs; KPI/event definitions; traceability.

## Quality checks
- [ ] Requirements are unambiguous, testable, prioritized and traceable; open decisions are visible.
- [ ] Work is traceable to the requested scope and project standards.
- [ ] Relevant project knowledge and documentation are updated.

## Boundaries
Do not invent policy or compliance requirements. Replace vague terms such as fast/secure with measurable criteria.
