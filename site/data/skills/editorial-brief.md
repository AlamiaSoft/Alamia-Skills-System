# Editorial Brief

Use before drafting articles, newsletters or other editorial assets.

## Inputs
topic, audience, reader action, channel, voice, sources and constraints

## Procedure
define reader problem and purpose; state a credible reader promise; select angle and scope; build outline around audience questions; list claims requiring evidence; specify CTA, tone, format, review and accessibility needs

## Outputs
 title, audience, purpose, angle, outline, evidence plan, CTA and acceptance checklist

## Quality checks
 a writer can start without guessing

## Boundaries
 do not fabricate audience research or expert input.
