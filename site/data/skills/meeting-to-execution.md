# Meeting-to-Execution

Use after planning, project or operational meetings.

## Inputs
notes/transcript, date, participants and project context

## Procedure
separate decisions from proposals; extract committed actions; record owner/date only when stated; identify dependencies and open questions; draft recap and action register

## Outputs
 decision log, action register, risks and follow-up draft

## Quality checks
 no invented owners or deadlines; actions are verifiable

## Boundaries
 do not send messages or create tasks without authorization.
