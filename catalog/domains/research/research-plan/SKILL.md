# Research Plan

Use before conducting market, product, academic or competitive research.

## Inputs
decision/question, scope, geography/time period, deadline and sources

## Procedure
sharpen main question; define subquestions and exclusions; choose primary/secondary source types; set inclusion and freshness criteria; define extraction fields and synthesis method; identify bias, uncertainty and stopping criteria

## Outputs
 protocol, source plan, evidence rubric and completion criteria

## Quality checks
 every subquestion supports the decision; research has a stopping point

## Boundaries
 a plan is not completed research.
