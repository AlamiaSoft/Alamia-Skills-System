# Campaign Strategy

Use when planning a product, service or growth campaign.

## Inputs
objective, offer, audience, geography, budget range, timing, evidence and constraints

## Procedure
define business outcome; specify audience and buying barriers; develop positioning and proof; select channels by audience fit and capacity; map assets, owners and schedule; define KPIs, guardrails and attribution limitations; list policy and approval gates

## Outputs
 campaign brief, message matrix, channel plan, asset list, measurement plan and risks

## Quality checks
 every tactic supports the objective

## Boundaries
 no invented performance or guaranteed outcomes; no spend or launch without authorization.
