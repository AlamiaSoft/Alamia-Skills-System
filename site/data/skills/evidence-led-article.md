# Evidence-Led Article

Use when producing research-informed editorial content.

## Inputs
approved brief, sources, audience, voice and citation style

## Procedure
map claims to sources; prefer primary/authoritative evidence; draft logically; distinguish facts, interpretation and recommendations; verify names, dates, numbers, quotes and links; flag unresolved claims

## Outputs
 article, source notes, open questions and editorial checklist

## Quality checks
 every material factual claim is supported; citations resolve

## Boundaries
 never invent sources, statistics, quotations or first-hand experience.
