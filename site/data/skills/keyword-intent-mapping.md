# Keyword Intent Mapping

Use before SEO content planning.

## Inputs
seed keywords, audience, geography, language and site context

## Procedure
normalize terms; classify intent (informational, navigational, commercial or transactional); cluster only when one page can satisfy the shared intent; identify audience need and page type; prioritize by relevance, business value, confidence and effort

## Outputs
 cluster table, representative query, variants, intent, reader need, page type, priority and unresolved questions

## Quality checks
 distinguish supplied metrics from hypotheses; never invent volume, CPC or difficulty

## Boundaries
 no ranking or traffic guarantees; do not claim live SERP research unless performed.
