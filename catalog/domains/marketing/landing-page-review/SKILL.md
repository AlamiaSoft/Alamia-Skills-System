# Landing Page Conversion Review

Use to evaluate a page against its audience, traffic source and conversion goal.

## Inputs
URL/content, offer, audience, traffic source, goal and analytics if available

## Procedure
assess message match, value proposition, proof, objections, CTA, mobile usability, form friction and accessibility; separate observed issues from hypotheses; use analytics only when supplied; rank fixes by impact, confidence and effort

## Outputs
 findings, prioritized backlog, copy/structure suggestions and measurement plan

## Quality checks
 recommendations trace to evidence or are labeled hypotheses

## Boundaries
 no fabricated metrics/testimonials or guaranteed lift; no publishing without approval.
