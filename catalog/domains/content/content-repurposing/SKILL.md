# Content Repurposing

Use to turn a report, article, webinar or video into channel-specific content.

## Inputs
approved source, target channels, audience, format limits and CTA

## Procedure
extract core claims; choose channel-specific angles; create native formats rather than simply truncating; preserve qualifications and attribution; flag new claims for verification

## Outputs
 channel matrix with hooks, drafts, CTA, source references and review notes

## Quality checks
 no unsupported additions or contradictory claims

## Boundaries
 do not publish or impersonate a person without authorization.
