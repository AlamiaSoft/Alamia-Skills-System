# Standard Operating Procedure Builder

Use to document an operational process for repeatable execution.

## Inputs
objective, owner, participants, trigger, systems, current practice and exceptions

## Procedure
define scope and outcome; identify roles; map prerequisites, steps, decisions and outputs; document exceptions, escalation, controls and records; flag policy/legal review; add version and review placeholders

## Outputs
 SOP with roles, procedure, decision points, exceptions and controls

## Quality checks
 a new team member can follow it; assumptions are marked

## Boundaries
 do not invent company policy or legal requirements.
