# Source Synthesis

Use to compare evidence and answer research questions.

## Inputs
question, source documents/URLs, dates and citation requirements

## Procedure
inventory sources; extract relevant claims; assess authority, method, recency and bias; compare agreement/conflicts; separate fact from inference; cite material claims and record gaps

## Outputs
 answer, evidence table, disagreements, limitations and source list

## Quality checks
 citations resolve and uncertainty is explicit

## Boundaries
 never invent sources, quotes, page numbers or exhaustive coverage.
