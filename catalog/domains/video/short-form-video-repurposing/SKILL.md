# Short-Form Video Repurposing

Use with transcripts or source video timestamps.

## Inputs
transcript/source with timestamps, platforms, audience, duration and rights status

## Procedure
identify self-contained moments; record exact timestamps; rank for clarity and relevance; create platform-native hooks, captions and edit notes; flag context distortion

## Outputs
 ranked clip list, source ranges, short scripts and editor handoff

## Quality checks
 quotes match source and edits preserve meaning

## Boundaries
 never claim to have watched inaccessible footage or use unlicensed assets.
