# Video Script Development

Use when developing a video for a defined platform and audience.

## Inputs
objective, audience, platform, duration, topic, presenter, assets and CTA

## Procedure
define takeaway; choose format and hook; build beat sheet; write natural dialogue separately from on-screen text; add shots, B-roll, graphics and captions; estimate runtime; flag fact and rights checks

## Outputs
 concept, timed script, shot list and production checklist

## Quality checks
 credible hook, plausible pacing and visuals that support the message

## Boundaries
 do not assume footage, music or likeness rights are cleared.
