# Email Campaign Builder

Use to plan email flows, campaigns and follow-ups.

## Inputs
goal, eligible segment, consent basis, offer, voice, platform, timing and suppression rules

## Procedure
define one action and metric; map entry/exit conditions and frequency; draft subject, preheader, body and CTA; verify claims, links, terms, personalization fallbacks and unsubscribe language; propose tests with a clear hypothesis

## Outputs
 sequence map, drafts, segmentation rules and QA checklist

## Quality checks
 no deceptive subject lines; consent and unsubscribe are explicit

## Boundaries
 do not scrape contacts, bypass consent or send without approval.
